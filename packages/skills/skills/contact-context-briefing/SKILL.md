# Contact Context Briefing

Before interacting with a known client, gather what Grisha knows.

## Workflow
1. Call `contact_briefing` with the contact name.
2. Present notes, related commitments and open questions.

## Rules
- Do not use fuzzy name matching for critical actions without confirming the
  contact's identity.
- Distinguish "known fact" from "guess" in the briefing.
